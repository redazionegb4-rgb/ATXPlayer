ATX Player 5.1 - Build 193

Compile repair after Build 192.

The previous approach incorrectly subclassed IOSVideoPlayerView and redeclared members
already owned by KSPlayer (titleLabel/backButton). Build 193 removes that architecture.

Now:
- IOSVideoPlayerView is used as a contained stock KSPlayer view, not subclassed.
- ATX controls live in a separate UIViewController overlay.
- No titleLabel/backButton naming collision.
- No override of KSPlayer non-open properties.
- No invalid IOSVideoPlayerView initializer override.
- No optional chaining on MediaPlayerProtocol.
- UIViewControllerRepresentable uses a non-private controller type, avoiding access-level errors.
- KSPlayer/KSMEPlayer remains the decoder.
- KSPlayer toolbar stays hidden.
- ATX title stays inside safe area.
- ATX controls: back, -10, play/pause, +10, AirPlay, fullscreen.
